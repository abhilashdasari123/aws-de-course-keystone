Module 1

What is Data Engineering & why it matters

Bedrock of the modern stack. If the pipe is wrong, dashboards, models, and GenAI all lie confidently.

Data engineering builds and operates systems that ingest, store, transform, and serve data — reliably, securely, and at sensible cost. You own the plumbing; consumers own the insights.

Garbage in, confident garbage out. A larger LLM or flashier BI tool cannot fix stale partitions or duplicate facts.

Course anchor: AWS (S3, Glue, Kinesis, Redshift). Industry parallels: Kafka, Snowflake, Airflow, dbt.

Module 2

Data Engineer vs Data Scientist vs Data Analyst

Same company, different questions. We use one running example: RetailCo and shelf replenishment for SKU-4421 at Store 12.

What is AskShelf? RetailCo’s internal GenAI chatbot for store managers. Managers ask natural-language questions (“How many cases of SKU-4421 should I reorder?”). AskShelf answers using a RAG index built from live stock tables, sales history, and supplier PDFs. If pipelines are stale or wrong, AskShelf still sounds confident — that’s a data engineering failure, not an “AI bug.”

Scenario: CEO wants better replenishment (fewer stock-outs and over-orders)

Live session: interview practice

      
After the modules — Easy / Medium / Hard (reveal answers in class).

      

        E · What does a data engineer do day to day?
        
Builds and maintains pipelines; monitors failures; models data; quality checks; cost and security.

      

      

        M · One OLTP and one OLAP example — why different DBs?
        
OLTP: place order (row store, few rows). OLAP: 3-year revenue (column scan). Mixing them slows apps and analytics.

      

      

        H · Last 5 seconds of orders + monthly revenue trends?
        
Stream to Kinesis for live view; land events in S3 + nightly Glue to Redshift/Athena; reconcile to source.
